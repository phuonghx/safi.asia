Capability areas

Eight areas of simulation-based training

Each area is built from complementary technologies, integrated into one program.

01

Clinical skills

Physical examination, nursing and core clinical procedures.

02

Procedural skills

Airway, vascular access, injections and other invasive procedures.

03

Surgical simulation

Open surgical skills, suturing and surgical team training.

04

Emergency and trauma

Trauma care, hemorrhage control and emergency response scenarios.

05

Critical care

Ventilation, ICU care and management of the deteriorating patient.

06

MIS and endoscopy

Laparoscopic and endoscopic skills, from box trainers to virtual reality.

07

Anatomy and orthopedics

Anatomical, bone and joint models for teaching and procedure practice.

08

Simulation centers and assessment

Center design, debriefing systems, OSCE and competency assessment.
